Before you go

01

What your ticket includes

General admission to the Calvin Harris residency night at Ushuaïa Ibiza's open-air stage, with access to the main arena, bars and food outlets. Tables and VIP areas are booked separately.

02

Age recommendations

Ushuaïa Ibiza operates a strict over-18s policy. A valid passport or ID card is required for entry regardless of age; anyone under 18 will be refused admission.

03

Best time to attend

Doors open in the late afternoon and the party runs into the night. Arrive early for the sunset sets and warm-up guests before Calvin's headline; weekends are the busiest.

04

Facilities

Full bar service, food outlets, cloakroom and cash machines on site. Outside food and drink are not permitted; free drinking water is available inside.

05

Accessibility

Ushuaïa Ibiza is largely step-free with accessible facilities. If you have specific access requirements, contact the venue ahead of your visit.

Getting there

Open in Google Maps
The venue
Ushuaïa Ibiza, Ctra. de Platja d'en Bossa 10, 07817 Ibiza, Spain. Open-air arena, capacity around 7,000.
By car
Head to Playa d'en Bossa on the southeast coast. Ushuaïa Ibiza is on Carretera Playa d'en Bossa. Two large paid car parks nearby — one directly across the road, one in the resort centre about 5–10 minutes' walk away.
By taxi
Taxis run from anywhere on the island and there is a taxi stand at the entrance. From Ibiza Town expect around 10 minutes; from San Antonio about 25 minutes.
By bus
The Disco Bus Line 3B connects San Antonio – Playa d'en Bossa – DC10 – Ushuaïa – Hï, running from around midnight to 6:30am for €5. There is a stop right outside Ushuaïa Ibiza.

Questions

What time does Calvin Harris play at Ushuaïa?

The open-air party usually runs from around 17:00 to 23:00. Doors open in the late afternoon with warm-up and guest DJs, and Calvin headlines in the evening. Arrive early to catch the sunset sets.

Is there an age restriction?

Yes. Ushuaïa Ibiza is strictly 18+. A valid passport or ID card is required for entry regardless of age; anyone under 18 will be refused admission.

Is there parking?

Parking around Playa d'en Bossa is limited and paid. We strongly recommend taxis or the Disco Bus, which stops right outside the venue, especially on peak nights.

Is it accessible to people with disabilities?

Ushuaïa Ibiza is largely step-free with accessible facilities. If you have specific access requirements, please contact the venue before your visit so they can assist on the night.

Can I bring food or drink from outside?

No. Outside food and drink are not permitted. There is full bar service and food available inside, and free drinking water is provided.

Can I get a refund on my tickets?

All sales are final; refunds are only granted if the event is cancelled or rescheduled. Please check the date and details carefully before booking.
